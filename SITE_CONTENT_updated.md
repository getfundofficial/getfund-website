## About (Component Visible Copy)

### Section Title
Who We Are

### Intro Paragraph
The Ghana Education Trust Fund is a statutory body established to provide financial resources for educational development.

### Mission
**Our Mission:** To mobilize and manage financial resources for the provision and expansion of educational infrastructure at all levels of education in Ghana

### Vision
**Our Vision:** To be a leading education trust fund in Africa, ensuring quality and accessible education for all Ghanaians

### Core Values
**Core Values:** Integrity, transparency, excellence, and commitment to transforming lives through education

### About GETFund
Since its establishment in the year 2000, the fund has remained committed to its mandate espoused in its governing legislation, Act 581. The object of the Fund is to provide funding to supplement government’s effort for the provision of educational infrastructure and facilities at all levels.

GETFund has since its inception remained a significant player in Ghana’s education sector not only by way of providing physical infrastructure, but also through the enhancement of our human capital, awarding scholarships to many deserving citizens.

The law establishing GETFund further emphasizes a distribution formula that must be approved by Parliament each year, requiring that some crucial factors related to education delivery and development are considered in the process of preparing the Formula.

**Relevant Factors:**
- Promotion of the study of Mathematics, Science and Technology
- Advancement of female education
- Reduction in the high level of illiteracy in historically disadvantaged areas
- Promotion of Vocational, Technical Education and Training
- Equitable allocation of funds to the Districts at the Pre-Tertiary level of education

## Who We Are

### Hero
- **Title:** About Us
- **Subheading:** Supporting the delivery of quality education to Ghanaians from basic to tertiary level

Since its establishment in the year 2000, the fund has remained committed to its mandate espoused in its governing legislation, the GETFund Act (Act 581), to provide funding to supplement government effort for the provision of educational infrastructure and facilities with the public sector.

GETFund has played a significant role in this country's education sector not only by way of physical infrastructure, but also through the enhancement of our human capital by way of scholarships.

### Vision & Mission
- **Our Vision:** To become a trusted public sector educational financing agency that sustainably manages assigned government resources
- **Our Mission:** To support the delivery of quality education to Ghanaians from the basic to tertiary level through dynamic funding policies aimed at ensuring equitable provision of essential resources for all levels of education
- **Our Values:** Integrity, transparency, excellence, and commitment to transforming lives through education

### The Issue of Educational Financing
- **The Cost Sharing Policy:** In Ghana, like most African countries, due to government's inability to finance tertiary education wholly, the idea of cost sharing was introduced in 1997 through the adoption of the Akosombo Accord (NCTE, 1998). This idea of cost sharing divided responsibility for university funding between the government (responsible for 70 percent of total funding) and 30 percent from three other sources including internal revenue-generation by the university, private donations and students' tuition fees.
- **Public Response & Agitation:** However, the introduction of cost sharing policies in the country yielded negative response from the public. The policy was not only politicized and attacked by Ghanaians but it also created severe inequalities making higher education the preserve of the socially privileged (Atuahene, 2006).

### Funding Formula
The law further stipulates that some crucial factors related to Education delivery and development are taken into account in the process of preparing this Formula. These relevant factors are:
- Promotion of the study of Mathematics, Science and Technology
- Advancement of female education
- Reduction in the high level of illiteracy in historically disadvantaged areas
- Promotion Vocational and Technical Education and Training
- Equitable allocation of funds to the Districts at the Pre-Tertiary level of education

### Leadership & Governance
- **Committed Board of Directors:** Our distinguished board members bring decades of experience in education, finance, and public administration to guide GETFund's strategic direction and ensure accountability in all our initiatives.
- **Strategic Oversight:** Ensuring alignment with national education priorities
- **Performance Monitoring:** Tracking impact and ensuring efficient resource utilization
- **Stakeholder Engagement:** Building partnerships across all education sectors

## FAQ

### Hero
Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about GETFund and our services

### FAQs (Full List)
- **Who Can Apply?** Ghanaian citizens who meet the academic requirements and have been admitted or are applying to approved institutions can apply for GETFund scholarships.
- **Which Universities Can One Apply To For GETFund Scholarship?** You can apply to any accredited university worldwide that offers programs aligned with Ghana's development priorities, particularly in Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, and other critical fields.
- **Can I Apply For A Scholarship Before Being Accepted Into A University?** Yes, you can apply for a GETFund scholarship before university admission, but you must provide evidence of application or conditional admission from your preferred institution.
- **How Do I Know If I Am Eligible To Apply?** Check the eligibility criteria on our scholarship portal. Generally, you need to be a Ghanaian citizen, have excellent academic records, and meet specific program requirements.
- **How Do I Submit My Application?** All applications must be submitted online through the GETFund scholarship portal at scholarships.getfund.gov.gh. Follow the step-by-step application process provided.
- **Can I Apply If I Am In My Second Year And Beyond?** GETFund scholarships are primarily for new students beginning their programs. Continuing students may not be eligible unless under special circumstances.
- **Can I Apply To Any Other Government Sponsoring Agency?** You cannot simultaneously hold scholarships from multiple government agencies. You must choose one sponsoring agency and declare this in your application.
- **What Is The Selection Process?** The selection process involves application review, document verification, interviews, and final approval by the GETFund Board based on merit and available resources.
- **What Disqualifies An Applicant?** Providing false information, failure to meet eligibility criteria, poor academic performance, or violation of scholarship terms can lead to disqualification.
- **What Are The Funding And Duration Details?** Funding covers tuition, living allowances, and other approved expenses. Duration depends on the program length, typically 1-2 years for Master's programs and 3-4 years for PhD programs.

### Transforming Educational Infrastructure
Since its inception, the core mandate of GETFund, per its enabling Act of parliament in 2000 (Act 581), has been to provide funding to supplement government effort for the provision of educational infrastructure and facilities within the public sector from the pre-tertiary to the tertiary level. In its efforts to discharge this important mandate, GETFund has chalked many successes and made significant contributions towards supporting education service delivery in the country.

### Key Achievements
- Expansion and improvement of academic facilities at tertiary, technical, and health institutions
- Construction of lecture theatres, libraries, laboratories, and hostel facilities
- Renovation of existing hostels and provision of essential laboratory equipment
- Significant infrastructural improvements at institutions like University of Cape Coast (UCC)

### Funding Highlights
- In 2004, over 74% of total allocations were directed toward tertiary infrastructure
- In 2018, Parliament approved GHC 924.8 million for disbursement, with 37.48% allocated to tertiary education
- In 2019, GHC 1.2 billion was approved, with tertiary education receiving the largest share of 26.4%

### Impact
GETFund's investments have enhanced the learning environment and strengthened Ghana's capacity to train a new generation of skilled professionals. The Fund remains committed to ensuring that every Ghanaian learner has access to quality tertiary education in a modern, well-resourced academic environment.

### Ongoing Commitment
Through sustained investments, GETFund continues to play a transformative role in Ghana's higher education landscape, ensuring access and quality for all.

## Community SHS Programme (Free SHS Intervention Project)

### Title
Free SHS Intervention Project

#### Infrastructure Intervention
The increase in enrollment figures necessitated investment in education infrastructure to support the Free and Quality Education Agenda. The infrastructure gap widened following the increase in secondary school enrollment numbers.

#### Educational Materials & Supplies
In addition to infrastructure intervention, additional supplies and learning materials were distributed to various schools (e.g. bunk beds, mattresses, teachers' tables & chairs, common room chairs, mono desks, etc).

#### Policy Objectives
- Removal of cost barriers
- Expansion of infrastructure
- Improvement in Quality and Equity
- Development of Employable Skills

#### Impact
Government has given full effect to SDG 4.1 with the implementation of a Free SHS Policy. The programme is anchored in the above pillars and has significantly increased access to secondary education in Ghana.

#### Role of GETFund
GETFund provides financial support for infrastructure, educational materials, and supplementary funding to institutions under the Ministry of Education, Scholarship Secretariat, and Student Loan Trust Fund.

### Lead Paragraphs
The Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFund) has signaled the immediate start of major infrastructure projects at Ambariya Senior High School (SHS) and Ambariya Community Primary School in the Tamale Metropolis, with a completion target of 18 months.
On the ground at both institutions, GETFund Administrator Mr. Paul Adjei announced the commencement of comprehensive facility upgrades designed to enhance the educational environment and support Ghana's educational development agenda.

At Ambariya Senior High School, the ambitious project includes:
- Construction of a modern 2-storey, 12-unit classroom block
- A fully equipped library facility
- Administrative block with modern offices
- Science laboratory complex
- Dormitory facilities to accommodate boarding students
- Complete water and sanitation systems

The Ambariya Community Primary School will benefit from:
- New classroom blocks to reduce overcrowding
- Kindergarten facilities
- Teachers' quarters
- Playground and recreational facilities
- Improved water and sanitation infrastructure

### Strategic Importance
Speaking at the project launch, Administrator Paul Adjei emphasized that these developments form part of GETFund's broader strategy to address infrastructure gaps in Ghana's education sector, particularly in underserved communities.
> "These projects represent our commitment to ensuring that every Ghanaian child, regardless of their geographical location, has access to quality educational infrastructure that supports effective learning," Mr. Adjei stated.

### Community Impact
The infrastructure improvements are expected to:
- Increase student enrollment capacity
- Improve learning outcomes through better facilities
- Attract and retain qualified teachers
- Serve as a model for other educational institutions in the region
- Boost community pride and engagement in education

### Timeline and Monitoring
With an ambitious 18-month completion timeline, GETFund has put in place robust monitoring mechanisms to ensure timely and quality delivery of the projects. Regular progress assessments will be conducted to maintain project momentum and address any challenges that may arise.
